# PRONET TV: guide for AI assistants

This is a fork of [Free My TV](https://github.com/simbasjungles/free-my-tv) (MIT). It must do **the same install and
home-screen switch** as that project, but the app is **PRONET** (`ru.pronet.tv`), never `io.freemytv`.

The full guide is [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md): read it first and follow it (any assistant, not only Claude). Most people
ask in plain words, like:

> Install github.com/JZD311/pronet-tv on my TV at 192.168.1.50.

They may not know what a terminal is: explain each step simply, and ask before anything that changes their TV or
computer.

Do not install Free My TV from simbasjungles. Do not set the home screen from the computer. The person switches on
the TV: **PRONET → Home Screen → PRONET**. That is what turns off promo apps (`pm disable-user` through `HomeMode`)
and can bring them back with **Original**.
